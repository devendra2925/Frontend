What puts them at risk

Habitat loss
Forests are cleared for farming, palm oil and roads. Animals are left with patches too small to breed in.
Poaching and trade
Ivory, horn, skins and live animals are sold illegally, and small populations cannot absorb the losses.
Fishing gear
Whales, porpoises and turtles drown in nets and lines meant for other catches.
Climate change
Warming shifts the habitats, food and seasons that species depend on, especially in mountains and oceans.

What helps

  • Choose certified products. Look for FSC wood and paper, MSC seafood and RSPO palm oil.
  • Support habitat protection. Give to conservation groups that buy, restore or patrol wild land.
  • Never buy wildlife products. Skip ivory, shell, fur and exotic pets, even when sold as souvenirs.
  • Share what you learn. Most people don’t know how few of these animals are left.
